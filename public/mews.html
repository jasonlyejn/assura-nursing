🩸 ABG Correction Rules (血气调整指南)
  • High PaCO₂ (Acidosis): Increase Minute Ventilation (↑ RR or ↑ Vt).
  • Low PaCO₂ (Alkalosis): Decrease Minute Ventilation (↓ RR or ↓ Vt).
  • Low PaO₂ (Hypoxemia): Increase FiO₂ and/or PEEP per ARDSNet table.
  • Metabolic Acidosis: Investigate perfusion/lactate/renal; avoid over-ventilation.
⚠ High Peak Pressure (PIP) Diagnostic Flow
  • High PIP + Normal Pplat (<30): Airway Resistance (Suction sputum, bite block, bronchodilator for spasm).
  • High PIP + High Pplat (>30): Decreased Compliance (Pneumothorax, pulmonary edema, ARDS, ETT in right bronchus).
  • Weaning RSBI: RSBI = RR / Vt (L) < 105 indicates readiness for extubation.
