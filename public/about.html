Founder's Message · 创办人心声与初心

"Why We Built Assura: Planning Care Around Your Home Without Compromising Clinical Fundamentals"

"Every family's home is unique. When patients return home after surgery or illness, they need professional, trained nurses who understand how to plan care around their specific living space—minimizing infection risks through strict hygiene and aseptic handling, while keeping patient safety and comfort at the center."

The Real Challenge We Solve (创办初心)

After hospital discharge, families frequently feel lost dealing with wound changes, feeding tubes, catheters, or bed transfers in a home environment that wasn't built like a clinic. Working adult children are stressed about causing infections or doing procedures incorrectly.

We founded Assura Nursing to solve this exact challenge: our nurses and caregivers are trained to evaluate the home setting, plan safe bedside workflows, and guide family members—all while strictly adhering to Malaysian Ministry of Health (MOH) and Nursing Board (LJM) clinical protocols.

Our Standard of Care · 专业护理标准与服务价值

Adapting to the Home, Upholding the Fundamentals

We believe effective home care is a balance of two essential skills: flexible environmental adaptation for family comfort, and uncompromising adherence to the core fundamentals of nursing care.

🏡

1. Home Environment Assessment & Planning

因地制宜·环境规划： Every home has unique spatial layouts, bed heights, and lighting. Our nurses assess the home setup, ergonomics, and accessibility to design a safe, practical daily care routine that fits your household.

🛡️

2. Uncompromising Nursing Fundamentals

恪守根本·降低感染风险： While adapting to the home, our trained nurses maintain professional handling and aseptic non-touch technique (ANTT) for wound dressings and catheterizations—managing a clean procedural field to minimize infection risks even in a home setting.

📋

3. Individualized Care Roadmap

量身定制·科学照护： Care plans are customized based on doctor discharge summaries, medication orders, mobility goals, and family schedules to ensure seamless recovery progression.

📊

4. Digital MEWS Vitals & Handover

数智记录·清晰交接： Real-time digital charting of blood pressure, SpO2, blood glucose, temperature, and wound photos, providing transparent daily records for families and doctors.

🎯
