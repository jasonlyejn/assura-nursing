🚨 Step-by-Step Home Emergency Guides

Immediate first-aid steps for families while waiting for medical assistance

Suspected Stroke (F.A.S.T.)

  • F (Face): Ask them to smile. Is one side drooping?
  • A (Arms): Ask them to raise both arms. Does one drift down?
  • S (Speech): Is speech slurred or difficult to understand?
  • T (Time to Call 999): Call 999 immediately. Note exact time.
🚨 Call 999 Now

Unresponsive / CPR Guide

  • 1. Check Response: Tap shoulders firmly: "Are you okay?"
  • 2. Call 999: Put phone on speaker.
  • 3. Chest Compressions: Push hard & fast at 100–120 beats/min.
  • 4. Use AED: Follow automated audio prompts.
🚨 Call 999 Now

Choking & Falls

  • Choking (Heimlich): 5 firm back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts.
  • Fall / Suspected Fracture: Do NOT move patient if neck/spine injury suspected.
  • Head Injury: Watch for pupil dilation, vomiting, or sudden drowsiness.
