24/7 Emergency & Clinical Response Guide

Critical protocols, emergency dispatch numbers, CPR steps, and Stroke F.A.S.T warning signs for families in Penang.

🚨 Emergency Numbers (Malaysia)

  • 🚑 National Emergency (Ambulance/Police): 999
  • 📱 From Mobile Phone: 112
  • 🩺 Assura 24/7 Clinical Care Hotline: 012-206 4868
  • 🏥 Penang General Hospital (HPP): 04-222 5333
  • 🏥 Seberang Jaya Hospital (HSJ): 04-382 7333

🧠 Stroke Warning Signs (F.A.S.T)

  • F — Face Drooping: Does one side of the face droop? Ask the person to smile.
  • A — Arm Weakness: Is one arm weak or numb? Ask them to raise both arms.
  • S — Speech Difficulty: Is speech slurred or strange? Ask them to repeat a simple sentence.
  • T — Time to Call 999: If any signs are present, immediately call 999 or proceed to the nearest emergency department.

❤️ Adult CPR Step-by-Step (Hands-Only CPR)

  1. Check for Responsiveness: Tap shoulders firmly and shout "Are you okay?".
  2. Call for Help: Shout for immediate assistance and dial 999 immediately.
  3. Position Hands: Place heel of one hand in the center of the chest, lace fingers with the other hand.
  4. Compress Hard & Fast: Push down at least 2 inches (5 cm) at a rate of 100 to 120 beats per minute (to the beat of "Stayin' Alive").
  5. Continue Until Help Arrives: Keep continuous compressions until the ambulance team or trained nurse takes over.
