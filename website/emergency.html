🧠 Stroke Warning Signs: F.A.S.T Guide

TIME IS BRAIN · 分秒必争

Recognize stroke symptoms immediately. A stroke occurs when blood supply to part of the brain is blocked. Act fast:

F
🥴
Face Drooping

Does one side of the face droop or feel numb? Ask the person to smile. Is the smile uneven or crooked?

A
💪
Arm Weakness

Is one arm weak, heavy, or numb? Ask them to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?

S
🗣️
Speech Slurred

Is speech slurred, garbled, or hard to understand? Ask them to repeat a simple sentence like "The sky is blue".

T
⏱️
Time to Call 999

If you see ANY of these signs, note the exact time symptoms started and call 999 immediately!

❤️ Adult Hands-Only CPR (Step-by-Step)

100 – 120 COMPRESSIONS / MIN

Hands-Only CPR can double or triple a person's chance of survival when sudden cardiac arrest occurs:

1 👋
Check Response

Tap shoulders firmly and shout loudly: "Are you okay?".

2 📞
Call 999 / 112

Shout for nearby help and dial 999 immediately on speakerphone.

3 👐
Position Hands

Place heel of one hand in center of chest, lace other hand on top.

4 ⚡
Compress Hard & Fast

Push down 2 inches (5cm) at 100–120 beats per minute.

5 🚑
Do Not Stop

Continue compressions until the ambulance crew arrives.
