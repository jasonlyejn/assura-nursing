Home Care Guides · 护理指南

When to call a home nurse vs. Hospital Emergency

🏡 Call Assura Home Nursing (24/7)

• Scheduled wound dressings & suture removals
• Catheter / Ryle's tube replacement
• IV drip, blood pressure & glucose monitoring
• Post-surgery recovery & bed-bound hygiene

🚨 Go to Hospital Emergency (999)

• Sudden chest pain, shortness of breath, or blue lips
• Sudden facial drooping or slurred speech (Stroke FAST)
• Uncontrolled profuse bleeding or severe head trauma
• High fever with active seizures
