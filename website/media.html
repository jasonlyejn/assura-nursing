❤️ Adult Hands-Only CPR & 110 BPM Metronome AHA / ERC LIFE-SAVING STANDARD

Hands-Only CPR Protocol

If an adult collapses, becomes unresponsive, and is not breathing normally, every second counts. Initiate continuous chest compressions immediately while waiting for the ambulance.

  • 1. Check & Call: Tap shoulders, shout "Are you OK?". Dial 999 or 112 on speakerphone.
  • 2. Position Hands: Heel of one hand on center of chest (lower breastbone), interlock other hand on top.
  • 3. Push Hard & Fast: Push down 2 inches (5 cm) at 100–120 beats per minute. Allow complete recoil.

📚 Patient & Family Clinical Home Care Guides Step-by-Step Practical Guidelines
🩹
Aseptic Wound Care & Dressing
Surgical, Bedsores & Diabetic Ulcers

Protect healing tissue and prevent bacterial infection using certified Aseptic Non-Touch Technique (ANTT).

  • 1. Wash hands thoroughly with antibacterial soap and prepare a clean sterile field.
  • 2. Cleanse wound gently with sterile Normal Saline from the cleanest area outward.
  • 3. Apply primary prescribed dressing (hydrocolloid / foam) and secure edges firmly.
  • 4. Keep dressing clean, dry, and intact (CDI). Change immediately if soiled or leaking.
⚠️ Infection Red Flags: Spreading redness >2cm, intense heat, foul odor, cloudy discharge, or patient fever >38°C.
🍼
Safe Ryle's / NG Tube Feeding
Aspiration Prevention & Bolus Protocol

Prevent fatal pulmonary aspiration pneumonia during enteral tube nutrition administration.

  • 1. Elevate patient's head of bed to at least 45° to 60° before starting feeds.
  • 2. Verify tube marking at nose and check gastric residual volume with a 50ml syringe.
  • 3. Administer feeding formula slowly via gravity (200–250ml over 20–30 minutes).
  • 4. Flush tube with 30–50ml warm boiled water post-feed to keep lumen patent.
  • 5. Crucial: Keep patient upright for 45–60 minutes after feeding.
⚠️ Stop Feeding Immediately If: Patient coughs persistently, vomits, experiences choking, or struggles to breathe.
💧
Foley Urinary Catheter Hygiene
CAUTI Prevention & Drainage Maintenance

Maintain strict hygiene to prevent Catheter-Associated Urinary Tract Infections (CAUTI).

  • 1. Keep urine drainage bag below bladder level at all times to prevent backflow.
  • 2. Perform gentle peri-care twice daily with mild soap and clean warm water.
  • 3. Ensure tubing is free of loops, kinks, or tension that can cause urethral trauma.
  • 4. Empty drainage bag when 2/3 full, avoiding contact between tap and container.
⚠️ Clinical Alert: Dark concentrated tea-colored urine, blood clots, sudden cessation of urine output, or lower abdominal pain.
🛌
Stroke Rehabilitation & Offloading
2-Hourly Turning & Contracture Prevention

Protect vulnerable pressure points and maintain limb function in bedridden or post-stroke patients.

  • 1. Enforce a strict 2-hourly turning schedule (Left lateral → Supine → Right lateral).
  • 2. Place supportive pillows between knees, under affected arm, and floating heels.
  • 3. Perform gentle passive Range of Motion (ROM) on hemiplegic limbs twice daily.
  • 4. Apply barrier moisturizing cream over bony prominences (sacrum, hips, ankles).
💡 Caregiver Tip: Pair regular turning with a 3-function electric bed and alternating ripple mattress for 100% bedsore prevention.
💊
Medication Safety & Standard Times
Standard Malaysian Clinical Dosing Schedule

Follow standard Malaysian hospital medication serving intervals to maintain therapeutic drug blood levels.

  • ⏰ OD (Once Daily): 07:00 or 12:00 (Morning / Midday)
  • ⏰ BD (Twice Daily): 06:00 / 12:00 or 18:00 / 22:00 (12-hour intervals)
  • ⏰ TDS (Three Times Daily): 06:00, 15:00, 22:00 (8-hour intervals)
  • ⏰ QID (Four Times Daily): 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, 22:00 (6-hour intervals)
  • ⏰ ON (Nightly): 19:00 or 22:00 before sleep
⚠️ Do Not Crush: Never crush Enteric-Coated (EC), Sustained-Release (SR/XR/CR), or sublingual tablets without pharmacist clearance.
